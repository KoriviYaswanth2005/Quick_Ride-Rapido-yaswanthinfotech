    24x7 support

    Reach support during every ride from the app.

    • Check the vehicle number and captain name before you board.
    • Share your trip with someone you trust.
    • Sit where you can hold on firmly, and wear a helmet.
    • Confirm the drop location before the ride ends.
    • Keep your documents up to date.
    • Confirm the rider's name before you start.
    • Use the emergency button if you feel unsafe.
    • Take breaks and avoid riding when you are tired.
