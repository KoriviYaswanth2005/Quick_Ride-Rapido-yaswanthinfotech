    How we got here

    We started with one simple idea: a fast, affordable bike ride should be available whenever you need it.

    Each new city brings its own traffic, routes and riders. We learn from every launch and adapt the service.

    Captain training, verification and in-ride support are built into the product from the start.

    Bike taxis, autos and cabs in one app, so you can pick the ride that suits your trip and budget.

    What we value

    Fair pricing

    Fares are shown before you book, with no surprise charges.

    Respect

    Riders and captains are treated with courtesy in every ride.

    Speed

    Short waits and quick trips, even in heavy traffic.
